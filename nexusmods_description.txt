[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
DragonWilds Ultra Editor is a standalone save editor for [i]RuneScape: Dragonwilds[/i]. Launch it and pick your character from a save browser, then freely edit your character, skills, inventory, storage and unlocks through a clean sidebar interface — with icons for every item so you can see exactly what you're editing. One Save button writes your changes and keeps automatic backups. It's a single Windows executable: no installer, no Python, no dependencies to set up.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close RuneScape: Dragonwilds (saves are read/written on exit — the game must not be running).
[*][b]Back up your save first.[/b] Your character saves live in:
[code]C:\Users\<YourName>\AppData\Local\RSDragonwilds\Saved\SaveCharacters[/code]
[*]Download and run [b]DragonWildsUltraEditor.exe[/b]. (Windows SmartScreen may warn on an unsigned exe — click "More info" → "Run anyway".)
[*]Pick your character from the picker screen (lists your saves newest-first with name/mode/level).
[*]Make your edits across the Overview, Character, Skills, Inventory, Storage, and Unlocks pages, then click [b]Save[/b].
[*]Launch the game and enjoy.
[/list]
[i]Tip: try it on a COPY of a save the first time.[/i]

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Character picker[/b] — jump straight to the save you want, sorted newest-first with name/mode/level/played-ago.
[*][b]Full inventory & storage editing[/b] — add, remove, bulk-fill, filter, and set slots/amounts, with an icon for every item.
[*][b]Gear & storage presets[/b] — apply ready-made loadouts in one click.
[*][b]Editable loadout[/b] straight from the Inventory page.
[*][b]Character & skills[/b] — change your name/GUID and edit skills.
[*][b]Unlock everything[/b] — recipes, buildings, mounts, picked-up items, and all 18 Sanguine Urns (max life pool).
[*][b]Reset[/b] Health, Status Effects, and more.
[*][b]One Save button[/b] writes every page's changes at once and keeps timestamped backups next to your save.
[*][b]Item icons[/b] throughout, name-based search/filter on all item lists, and Light & Dark themes.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Windows 10/11.
[*]A copy of RuneScape: Dragonwilds with at least one character save.
[*]No additional software required — everything is bundled in the exe.
[/list]
